Different from an MSP

Your IT provider runs technology. This review helps leadership govern the risk.

Many providers manage devices, accounts, software, backups, monitoring, and support. Leadership still needs to know which business or mission risks matter most, what the provider contract actually covers, what remains the organization’s responsibility, and how decisions will be coordinated across multiple parties.

The service does not grade or replace your provider. It produces an independent, evidence-labeled map of responsibilities and unresolved questions so leadership can make better decisions and have more productive provider conversations.

F-03

Backup recovery responsibility has not been confirmed

Address First
Risk theme Business continuity Evidence Customer-stated Owner status Not confirmed
Scenario
A ransomware event or administrator error makes shared files unavailable, but leadership does not know which cloud data is backed up or who is responsible for restoration.
Potential business effect
Lost access to customer, donor, finance, or program records; delayed operations; inability to confirm when service can resume.
Evidence basis
Customer-stated. IT-provider responsibility not confirmed from available information.
Current responsibility
Shared assumption between operations leadership and the IT provider; no named recovery decision owner.
Recommended action
Request written confirmation of systems covered, backup frequency, retention, restoration responsibilities, testing history, and customer-retained duties.
Evidence of completion
Written provider response, named internal owner, documented restoration contact, and agreed recovery test or verification step.

When this service is useful
