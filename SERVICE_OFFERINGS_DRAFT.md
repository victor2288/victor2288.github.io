# Outcome-Driven Public Service Offerings — Draft

> Status: Draft for founder review. This public-facing refinement does not replace canonical project files until formally accepted.

## Core Position

**MSPs and IT providers operate and secure technology. Victor Yu Cybersecurity Advisory gives leadership an independent, documented view of priority risks, responsibility boundaries, provider assumptions, and the actions that should happen next.**

The paid product is not a conversation, checklist, or generic assessment. It is a fixed-scope leadership decision package built from the customer’s actual systems, accounts, AI tools, providers, personnel roles, and operating dependencies.

## Offer 1 — Cybersecurity Risk and Operations Snapshot

**Founding-client price: $495**

### Best for
Leadership that needs a fast, bounded answer to:
- What could materially disrupt us?
- Which systems, accounts, and information matter most?
- Where is ownership unclear?
- What should we do during the next 30 days?

### Customer receives
1. **Critical Systems, Sensitive Information, and Accounts List** — up to 10 items
2. **Cybersecurity Risk Snapshot** — up to 5 priority concerns, including AI-related concerns when material, each with scenario, business effect, evidence basis, responsible party, priority, recommended action, and limitation
3. **Unclear-Responsibility List** — up to 5 ownership, provider-coverage, or AI-use questions
4. **One-Page Cybersecurity Risk and Leadership Priority Brief**
5. **30-Day Action Checklist** — up to 5 actions with accountable party and evidence of completion
6. **20-minute findings call**

### Customer outcome
Leadership can identify its most important near-term risks, understand which responsibilities need clarification, and assign a limited set of practical actions without buying another tool or commissioning a broad assessment.

### Standard scope
One organization, one agreed environment, up to 10 systems/accounts/services, up to 5 responsibility holders/providers, one 45-minute leadership session, up to 2 participants, no provider interviews, and no technical access or testing.

## Offer 2 — Cybersecurity Risk, Responsibility and Resilience Review

**Founding-client price: $950**

### Best for
Leadership that needs a documented operating view of:
- Business or mission impact
- Critical systems and sensitive information
- Internal and provider responsibilities
- Incident contacts and decision roles
- Provider questions and unconfirmed coverage
- A sequenced 30/60/90-day plan

### Customer receives
1. **Cybersecurity Risk and Leadership Summary**
2. **Critical Systems, Sensitive Information, Accounts, and AI Tools Register** — up to 20 items
3. **Cybersecurity and AI Responsibility Map**
4. **Provider and AI Vendor Coverage Clarification List** — up to 3 major providers
5. **Priority Risk and Responsibility Findings Register** — normally 8–12 findings
6. **Incident Contact and Escalation Sheet**
7. **Questions to Take Back to Providers**
8. **30/60/90-Day Leadership Action Plan**
9. **45-minute findings presentation**

### Customer outcome
Leadership receives a documented risk picture, named ownership, clearer provider and AI-vendor boundaries, an incident coordination structure, and a practical roadmap that can guide internal work, budgets, and provider follow-up.

### Standard scope
One organization, one agreed environment, up to 20 systems/accounts/services, up to 10 responsibility holders, up to 3 major providers, up to 3 short redacted documents, one 75-minute working session, factual confirmation, and one findings presentation. No direct provider interviews or technical testing.

## Finding Standard
Every material finding includes:
- Clear title
- Evidence classification
- Factual basis or customer statement
- Plain-language risk scenario
- Potential effect on operations, money, sensitive information, customer/donor trust, or mission
- Current stated owner
- Responsibility issue
- Recommended accountable party
- Recommended action
- Time category
- How completion can be confirmed
- Scope or evidence limitation

## Shared Exclusions
No penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, technical-control validation, formal compliance assessment, certification, IT-provider audit, legal or insurance advice, remediation, managed monitoring, emergency response, or guaranteed outcomes.


## Included AI Security Review
AI security review is included within both packages when AI use is relevant and remains subject to the normal system, finding, provider, and effort limits.

The review may cover:
- Public and enterprise AI tools in active use
- AI features embedded in email, office suites, CRM, fundraising, finance, meeting, and productivity systems
- Sensitive or confidential information entered into AI tools
- Approved, prohibited, and ungoverned AI use cases
- Account ownership, administrator access, connected applications, and data permissions
- Human-review requirements for AI-generated work
- AI vendor and IT-provider responsibility boundaries
- Incident ownership for exposed information, unauthorized use, unreliable output, or compromised AI-connected accounts

Customer outcomes may include an AI tool inventory, AI-use responsibility entries, AI-related findings, vendor clarification questions, and prioritized actions such as an approved-tool list, prohibited-data rule, human-review requirement, or incident escalation owner.

This is a governance and security review. It does not include AI model testing, adversarial red-teaming, prompt-injection testing, bias testing, algorithm validation, legal compliance certification, secure AI implementation, or a guarantee that AI outputs are accurate or safe.
