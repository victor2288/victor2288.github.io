Visitor privacy notice

How this website handles visitor information.

Last updated July 2026

Current website operation

This draft website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, user accounts, or an online database for inquiries.

The inquiry form prepares an email in your device's email application. The website itself does not upload or store the form contents.

Information you choose to send

If you send an inquiry by email, the information may include your name, organization, email address, selected service, and the business or responsibility issue you describe. That information may be used to evaluate service fit, respond to your inquiry, prepare a proposal, maintain necessary business records, or address a security or legal issue.

Information you should not send

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, API keys, private keys, payment credentials, raw personal records, medical information, full configuration exports, system images, database copies, unrestricted administrator access, privileged legal materials, or confidential incident evidence through the inquiry form or ordinary email.

AI and marketing use

Confidential inquiry information will not be intentionally submitted to an unapproved AI system or reused for marketing, testimonials, case studies, demonstrations, or model training without separate authorization.

Third parties and email delivery

Your email provider, internet provider, device software, and the recipient's email service may process message data under their own terms. Email is not guaranteed to be confidential or secure. Use only summary-level, non-secret information during initial contact.

No legal or compliance certification

This notice describes the intended operation of the current draft website. It is not legal advice and does not establish compliance with any specific privacy law, contract, or industry standard.
